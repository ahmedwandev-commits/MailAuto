{{ icon('download') }}Shift allowance (بدل ورديات)

How the Shift allowance sheet counts days - counts only, no money. It reads the HR attendance file and the portal's schedule.

Which shift is it?

A “shift worker” is somebody who has shifts in the schedule of that month. Without a planned shift, a check-in at the start of a shift counts as that shift (the times in the table); otherwise leaving at or after the time above is Evening, a check-in in the night window is Night, anything else Morning. A person with no shifts in the schedule (normal day) is not counted. Managers are counted for the morning shift and holidays only (no evening or night).

Holidays

A day worked on a weekly rest day (HR day type “weekly rest”) or on an official holiday (a date in Holidays below, or any other non-working HR day type) counts as «عطلة», whatever the time - for employees and managers. Both are written عطلة in the file.
